Kudos to You is an achievement that requires the player to obtain all available Kudos by helping out the Varrock Museum. To check remaining Kudos points to gain, speak with the information clerk in the north west corner of the museum.

A player can earn a maximum of 198 kudos in the Varrock Museum activity through 5 different methods:

  • By cleaning finds (50 kudos)
  • By answering the natural history quiz about museum exhibits given by Orlando Smith in the basement (or Mark Brodie, who is found at the same location, if The World Wakes is already completed) (28 kudos)
  • By relating details of certain completed quests to Historian Minas (95 kudos)
  • By completing the statue of Dahmaroc for the first time (10 kudos)
  • By completing the following requirements and talking to Mr Mordaut in Varrock Museum (15 kudos)
